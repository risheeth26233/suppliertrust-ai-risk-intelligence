# Design reference field notes

Research notes for the ten requested references. These are observations from public pages and documentation, not claims about logged-in product behavior. Access was checked on 19 November 2026; websites can change.

| Reference | What was accessible | Transferable principle | Limits of observation |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Apple](https://www.apple.com) | Public marketing site and [Human Interface Guidelines: Motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion/) | Motion should clarify hierarchy and change; use a small set of purposeful transitions, stable spatial relationships, and a reduced-motion alternative. Tell the product story through the content itself. | The research pass did not instrument every scroll, hover, or timing curve on the marketing site. Treat timing values as project decisions, not Apple specifications. |
| [Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com) | Public site plus the [design blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/core-tabs-redesign/) account of its tab redesign | Test navigation variants with users; make labels and active states consistent; use progress and short steps to make a complex first task understandable. For business onboarding, motivation means clarity and completion feedback, not game rewards. | No signed-in lesson or first-run onboarding was exercised. The adaptation to enterprise workflows is a recommendation. |
| [Linear](https://linear.app) | Public product pages and indexed official docs/changelog on [selection](https://linear.app/docs/select-issues), [keyboard shortcuts help](https://linear.app/changelog/2021-03-25-keyboard-shortcuts-help), and [desktop navigation](https://linear.app/changelog/2021-09-23-desktop-navigation) | Keep navigation predictable; preserve dense information with alignment and restrained emphasis; make keyboard commands discoverable and selections unambiguous. | The signed-in product and all docs pages were not accessible in this pass; some documentation pages were index-only. Do not claim the entire live keyboard model was tested. |
| [Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com) | Public work, discipline and sector categories, and selected project pages | Pair a distinctive display face with highly readable body text; use size, weight, measure, and whitespace to establish editorial hierarchy. Identity can be expressed through typographic choices without reducing legibility. | No proprietary font or exact brand system was extracted. Typeface pairing is project-specific. |
| [MUJI](https://www.muji.com) | The requested regional site showed a maintenance notice; [MUJI US](https://www.muji.us) was accessible for basic catalog/navigation | Remove decoration that competes with the task; keep product and category organization visible. Minimalism still needs enough labels, context, and useful density. | The requested site was unavailable, and the alternate regional storefront is not interchangeable. Search/filter interactions were not tested. |
| [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com) | Public page with a JavaScript-disabled notice and visible search categories/fields | Put the main search task in a clear starting point; make category/filter selection and result state easy to distinguish; provide an obvious way to clear constraints. | Dynamic filter, result, and booking interactions were not exercised. These are implementation guidelines, not observed results behavior. |
| [Aesop](https://www.aesop.com) | Public page with a limited/offline functionality notice and portions of its menu/product structure | Use quiet color, restrained composition, and concise product context to create a calm reading rhythm. | The site warned that functionality was limited; no product interaction or transition behavior is claimed. Avoid copying art direction or assets. |
| [Notion](https://www.notion.com) | Public homepage and product story blocks | Organize work into readable modules; provide contextual actions near the information they affect; let detail appear progressively rather than presenting every control at once. | No logged-in dashboard was accessed. Modular homepage sections are the observed basis; dashboard behavior is an adaptation. |
| [Stripe](https://stripe.com) | Public marketing page redirected by region and presented product/CTA sections | Connect a clear proposition to an understandable product illustration and one primary next step. Use section changes to support the story, not to distract from it. | Regional redirect and locale differed from the requested default; no scroll animation was instrumented. Do not copy illustrations or exact layouts. |
| [Framer](https://www.framer.com) | Public [animation lesson](https://www.framer.com/academy/lessons/framer-animations-overview/) and [reduced-motion troubleshooting](https://www.framer.com/help/articles/troubleshooting-animation-issues/) | Reuse a small set of states such as hover, press, appear, and scroll; use matching properties for transitions; keep text motion restrained and honor reduced motion. | This is public product guidance, not a benchmark of every published site or a guarantee of a particular runtime cost. |

## Application guidance

- Use observed ideas as questions to ask: Is the primary task obvious? Does selected state read without color alone? Can a keyboard user reach it? Is an animation carrying information?
- Do not inherit a reference's brand colors, copy, typeface, illustrations, or page composition.
- If a source is inaccessible, say so and use an accessible official document or alternate page only as clearly labeled supporting evidence.
